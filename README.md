# Firelytics AI
### NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 | Theme: Flame in Freefall

## Project Summary

Firelytics AI is an AI-powered research assistant designed to help scientists, students, and space enthusiasts explore research related to combustion and fire behaviour in microgravity. It connects users with NASA's Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) investigation records and helps them search, understand, and compare scientific research.

Using AI-assisted search and evidence-grounded analysis, the platform makes scientific investigation metadata, research documents, and available experimental datasets easier to explore. Users can ask questions in natural language, examine relevant research evidence, and compare investigations without having to navigate large collections of scientific information manually.

Firelytics AI addresses an important challenge in space exploration: understanding fire behaviour in microgravity is essential because fire risks aboard spacecraft can threaten crew safety, equipment, and mission success. By making existing research more accessible and easier to interpret, our platform supports faster knowledge discovery and helps users build a stronger understanding of flame behaviour in freefall.

**Our goal:** Make scientific knowledge about fire in space easier to discover, understand, and explore.
